## The distinction

A missing transcriptomic measurement does not show that a protein is inactive. A missing close homolog does not prove that a function is absent. In both cases, the evidence was not observed or was not available.

## Why annotation systems need this distinction

A useful annotation record should distinguish a positive observation, a result that argues against a claim, and a measurement that was never available. Collapsing the last two into a zero can make a system sound more certain than the data justify.
